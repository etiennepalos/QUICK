Independent electronic jets and continuous support majorants
===========================================================

Scope and source convention
---------------------------
quick_electrostatic_jet_module is a new independent CPU implementation.
Published ESP/EFIELD/EFG, RESP, applied-field and continuum code is not
called or modified by it. The supplied real symmetric density is fixed.
For unrestricted calculations the caller supplies D_alpha+D_beta. Native
Cartesian primitive coefficients must be passed intact, including original
primitive and contraction normalization; no spherical transformation or
renormalization occurs. The upper triangle D(nu,mu), mu>=nu, defines the
contraction; off-diagonal pairs carry a factor two. owned(mu) partitions the
higher AO index. Zero input density or primitive coefficients are exact
zeros; otherwise there is no intentional primitive/density cutoff.

V_el=-sum(D*I); E=-grad_C(V_el); G_ij=dE_i/dC_j;
H_ijk=dG_ij/dC_k. All coordinates are bohr, the potential is Eh/e, and
each derivative adds one inverse bohr. Nuclear, external point-charge,
direct applied-field and continuum-reaction contributions are absent.
The density may contain their SCF polarization response. Classical sources
must be added directly at query points by the higher-level controller.

Interfaces and transaction contract
-----------------------------------
electronic_jet_cpu(nb,na,maxprim,nc,ang,centers,expo,coeff,atoms,D,
                   npoints,points,owned,V,E,G,H,status,ierr)
electronic_cell_majorants_cpu(nb,na,maxprim,nc,ang,centers,expo,coeff,atoms,D,
                   ncells,lower,upper,owned,component_bounds,status,ierr)

All arrays follow the existing raw Hessian geometry/basis layout: ang(3,nb),
one-based centers(nb), expo/coeff(maxprim,nb), atoms(3,na), D(nb,nb), and
owned(nb) in {0,1}. Jet results are V(np), E(3,np), G(3,3,np), H(3,3,3,np).
Bounds are component_bounds(35,ncells), with box endpoints (3,ncells).
Point cells lower=upper are allowed. Inputs must be finite and angular
momentum is limited to Cartesian s/p/d/f, sum(ang(:,mu))<=3.

Both raw calls are ADDITIVE and transactional. Outputs change only if the
whole call succeeds; a later overflow does not expose partial contractions.
Status resets on every call, including errors. ierr=46 is dimension error,
55 invalid numeric/range data, 56 allocation/backend failure. These raw
routines have no retained-density state, MPI collectives, GPU dispatch,
SCF work, screening threshold, or internal cache. Callers must synchronize
rank-local errors before reducing results. Sum positive component bounds
across ownership partitions BEFORE forming a tensor norm.

Packing and symmetry
--------------------
JET_COMPONENT_POWERS(3,35) and JET_COMPONENT_RANK(35) are public constants.
Packing is rank0 at1, rank1 at2:4, rank2 at5:10, rank3 at11:20, rank4
at21:35. Within a rank, x power descends and then y power descends; z is
the remaining degree. Thus rank2 is xx,xy,xz,yy,yz,zz and rank3 is
xxx,xxy,xxz,xyy,xyz,xzz,yyy,yyz,yzz,zzz. Rank4 similarly has15 components.
Each computed G/H component is expanded identically to all permutations.
jet_rank_norm(component_bounds,rank) returns the full Frobenius norm using
r!/(nx!ny!nz!) multiplicities; invalid rank/nonfinite input returns -1.

Hermite/F10 construction
-----------------------
For each primitive pair, p=alpha+beta, P=A+beta/p*(B-A), and
K=exp[-alpha*beta/p*|A-B|^2]. The density/normalization prefactor is
w*ca*cb*(2*pi/p)*K. Signed one-dimensional Hermite coefficients use
E_new(t)=E_old(t-1)/(2p)+(P-A or P-B)*E_old(t)+(t+1)*E_old(t+1).
Their positive recurrence replaces each displacement by its absolute value,
which bounds the magnitude of every signed coefficient by induction.

For an entire support box, qmax_i=max(|P_i-lower_i|,|P_i-upper_i|),
qmin_i=distance(P_i,[lower_i,upper_i]), and Tmin=p*sum(qmin_i^2).
Boys F_m is positive and monotone decreasing. Seed positive Coulomb bounds
with (2p)^m*F_m(Tmin); propagate
Rbar_(t+e_i)^m=qmax_i*Rbar_t^(m+1)+t_i*Rbar_(t-e_i)^(m+1).
For the jet, use signed seeds (-2p)^m*F_m(p*|P-C|^2) and q=P-C.
Observation derivatives contribute (-1)^rank to the integral. Consequently
the packed electronic V,E,G,H use contraction signs -, -, +, -.

Cell component bounds sum the positive Hermite/Coulomb contraction times
the absolute full density prefactor. No density cancellation is used.
Ranks3 and4 Frobenius norms provide M3 and M4 over the ENTIRE box support.
For f/f pairs the required maximum Boys order is10. Compact descending-m
recurrence needs two degree-10 Cartesian tetrahedra, 2*286 doubles, rather
than a full four-dimensional tensor. At layer m only Cartesian degrees
<=order-m are needed. The degree-ordered prefix has
nactive=(order-m+1)*(order-m+2)*(order-m+3)/6 entries. First predecessors
lie in the completed previous degree prefix, and second predecessors have
one further degree less; the initial layer reads no predecessor. Every
active entry is assigned before use. Thus only the active prefix is checked
and copied, with no full-layer clearing or initial previous-layer clearing.
The final result is explicitly zeroed before copying its active prefix,
preserving unused output-tail zeros. Seed/Boys arithmetic, contraction
ordering, finite checks on computed entries and range status are unchanged.
Highest-order Boys functions use the
positive-term expansion below T=32 and stable downward recurrence; above
32 an erf seed and upward recurrence are used. This new routine shares
the mathematics of the independent Hessian route but changes no old path.

What the status does and does not establish
------------------------------------------
The positive recurrence is a real-arithmetic component inequality evaluated
in ordinary FP64. It is not outward-rounded, does not validate Boys values,
and does not bound accumulation/anchor error. no_intentional_screen is true;
numerical_budget_known and certified are always false. This remains true
even if every range counter is zero. No unknown error is assigned zero.

primitive_pairs counts nonzero input primitive pairs attempted. A Gaussian
or final primitive prefactor below the smallest normal number increments
prefactor_underflow_count and sets underflow_detected. An underflowed Boys
value similarly increments boys_underflow_count. Zero hardware prefactors
cannot contribute further, and such range loss is explicitly reported.
These detected events do NOT exhaust every possible intermediate underflow
or establish a residual bound. An intermediate overflow/nonfinite result
returns error55 with unchanged outputs. A certified/full numerical error
mode remains unavailable until validated special functions, scaled range
handling, anchor intervals and outward-rounded arithmetic exist.

POU and resource contract for the controller
-------------------------------------------
The experimental controller uses one regular cover per successful batch,
degree2 or3 local potential polynomials, compact C3 product weights, and
their exact first/second quotient derivatives. Evaluate a scalar potential
and differentiate it coherently; do not blend independently computed E/G.
Majorants must cover each full weight support, including overlap. Tensor
H anchors are direct; interpolated H has no supplied guarantee. A constant
potential gauge offset must cancel from E/G through weight identities.

Use explicit caps on anchors, depth, overlap, memory and setup time. The
initial mathematical pilot used512 anchors. The production default permits
600 cumulative attempted anchors, enough for levels0..3 with1+8+64+512=585
anchors; a regular cover has at most8 active supports per query. Balanced
octrees require a separate verified overlap contract. The memory control is
a conservative additional-work estimate used for route selection, not a
hard process-RSS or pre-allocation bound: the public API's transactional
output buffers and full-D copy are allocated before the controller check;
direct fallback has required baseline API buffers and resident QUICK state
lies outside this additional-work estimate. Preparation time is checked
cooperatively between batches of at most16 anchors or8 support boxes; it is
not a preemptive deadline for one integral batch or direct fallback.
If tolerances cannot be met at caps, report failure or select one direct
whole-frame route; do not silently mix direct/interpolated per-query values
across a boundary. Indicator, real-arithmetic analytic_majorant, and an
unavailable certified total-error mode must remain distinct. A provided
anchor-error budget is an additional conditional input, never inferred zero.
A fixed domain does not freeze query-dependent level selection across
independent API calls. Derivative checks must retain the same cover, anchors
and density, or call the exposed POU evaluator on an explicit frozen cover.
