Focused initial comparison, checked 2026-10-15. This is not a systematic
priority search and does not establish novelty of the proposed combination.

1. Babuska and Melenk, The partition of unity method, International Journal
   for Numerical Methods in Engineering 40 (1997), 727-758.
   https://doi.org/10.1002/(SICI)1097-0207(19970228)40:4<727::AID-NME86>3.0.CO;2-N
   Publisher abstract inspected. Smooth approximation spaces assembled with
   partitions of unity, convergence analysis and error estimation are
   established ingredients. Do not claim their introduction here.

2. Lekien and Marsden, Tricubic interpolation in three dimensions,
   International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering 63 (2005),
   455-471, DOI 10.1002/nme.1296.
   https://www.cds.caltech.edu/~marsden/bib/2005/08-LeMa2005/LeMa2005.pdf
   Author-hosted primary paper inspected. Local cubic interpolation with
   derivative data and continuity properties predates this project. Its
   tensor-product corner interpolant differs from the present compactly
   blended total-degree Taylor polynomials; that distinction is not itself
   evidence of a useful new method.

3. Fong and Darve, The black-box fast multipole method, Journal of
   Computational Physics 228 (2009), 8712-8725.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2009.08.031
   https://mc.stanford.edu/cgi-bin/images/f/fa/Darve_bbfmm_2009.pdf
   Publisher/author-hosted primary paper inspected. Interpolation-based
   acceleration of kernel sums with controlled approximation is established.
   A future performance claim needs the current native direct CUDA baseline
   and relevant alternatives; a comparison only with a slow CPU is inadequate.

4. Kowalewski, Larsson and Heryudono, An adaptive interpolation scheme for
   molecular potential energy surfaces (2016 preprint).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.09751
   Author abstract inspected. Adaptive node refinement using local error
   estimates and partition-of-unity interpolation has molecular precedents.
   Their object is a nuclear-coordinate potential-energy surface, whereas
   the current object is a fixed-density observation-coordinate ESP.

5. Gharibnejad et al., A multi-center quadrature scheme for the molecular
   continuum, Computer Physics Communications 263 (2021), 107889.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2021.107889
   https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=930736
   Primary paper inspected. Smooth atomic partitions and complementary
   long-range weights are already used in molecular integral/Poisson work.
   Their quadrature construction is not the present query interpolation,
   but precludes a broad claim that smooth partitions for electrostatic
   quantities are new.

Present defensible scope: an experimental QUICK implementation combining
coherent electronic V/E/G/H anchors, ordinary-FP full-density support
majorants through F10, complete partition derivative terms, resource caps
and whole-frame direct fallback. Demonstrated performance, bound usefulness
and a narrower literature comparison remain necessary before presenting a
distinct numerical-method contribution. No floating-point certification is
implemented. Preserve negative results and unavailable numerical budgets.
